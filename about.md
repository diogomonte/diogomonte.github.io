I’m a Software Engineer with a career that began in 2009, developing applications across diverse domains including media, sports, logistics, and IoT. This experience has taught me to quickly adapt to different business contexts and translate complex requirements into maintainable, high-quality software.

I’m passionate about writing clean, readable, and testable code. By practicing Test-Driven Development (TDD) and incremental development, I ensure that changes are small, manageable, and low-risk, improving code quality and supporting safe refactoring. I prioritize high test coverage, clear documentation, and continuous integration to provide fast feedback and reduce technical debt.

Knowledge sharing and collaboration are central to how I work. I conduct thorough code reviews and mentor team members, helping teams adopt best practices and collectively improve their software development processes. I regularly ask myself:

Is my code easy to maintain?
Is it easy to change?
Is it easy to read and understand?
Does it clearly express intent?

I thrive in teams that value strong connections and mutual support, where members inspire each other and foster an environment of continuous learning. My goal is to contribute to teams that deliver reliable, maintainable, and high-quality software while enabling everyone to grow as engineers.
