About

I'm a software engineer based in Copenhagen, Denmark. Over the past 15 years I've built mobile apps and backend platforms, and spent much of that time on the structure underneath them: how code is organised, how teams agree on patterns, and how systems stay maintainable as they grow.

Today I focus on architecture and on how AI agents change the way we build software. I care about clean code, honest product design, and shipping things that work for real people.
