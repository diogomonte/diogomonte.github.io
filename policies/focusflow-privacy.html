FocusFlow — Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2025

iOS Privacy
Effective: April 1, 2025

Your privacy is important. This policy explains what data FocusFlow collects, how it is used, and your rights as a user.

1. Data We Collect

FocusFlow is designed to work entirely on-device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to external servers.

  • All session data (timers, streaks, settings) is stored locally on your device.
  • No account creation is required.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs are embedded in the app.

2. iCloud Sync (optional)

If you enable iCloud sync in Settings, your session data will be stored in your personal iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit framework. This data is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy. Diogo Monte has no access to your iCloud data.

3. In-App Purchases

FocusFlow uses Apple's in-app purchase system for the Pro upgrade. We do not receive your payment details. Apple processes all transactions.

4. Notifications

The app may request permission to send local notifications (e.g., "Session complete!"). These notifications are generated and delivered entirely on your device. No notification data leaves your device.

5. Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and notify users via an in-app notice.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out at hello@diogomonte.dev.
