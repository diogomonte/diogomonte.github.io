IAgro — Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2025

AndroidPrivacy
Effective: April 7, 2025

IAgro is a local-only farm finance manager for Android. This policy explains our data practices — which are as simple as it gets: we collect nothing.

1. About IAgro

IAgro helps farmers and rural producers track their finances — from animal-related costs and pricing to general farm expenses. The app is designed to run entirely on your device, with no internet connection required or used at any point.

2. Data Collection

IAgro does not collect any personal information. All data you enter — including financial records, animal entries, and pricing information — is stored locally on your device and never leaves it. There are no user accounts, no registration, and no sign-in required.

3. Internet Access

IAgro does not access the internet. The app requests no network permissions and makes no outbound connections of any kind. Your data stays on your device, always.

4. Permissions

IAgro requires no special device permissions to operate. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or any other sensitive system resource.

5. No Third-Party SDKs

IAgro contains no analytics libraries, crash reporting tools, advertising SDKs, or any other third-party services. There is nothing in the app that could transmit your data to anyone.

6. Data Deletion

Uninstalling IAgro from your device permanently removes all locally stored data. You can also clear all data from within the app via its settings.
