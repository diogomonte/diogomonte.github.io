WalletLite — Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2025

AndroidPrivacy
Effective: April 1, 2025

WalletLite is a fully offline expense tracker. This policy explains our minimal data practices.

1. Data Collection

WalletLite stores all data (transactions, categories, budgets) locally on your device using Room database. No data is ever sent to external servers. We collect nothing.

2. Permissions

  • Storage (optional): Required only if you export data to a CSV file on your device.
  • Notifications (optional): For budget-limit alerts, generated locally.

All permissions are optional and can be revoked at any time in Android Settings.

3. No Third-Party SDKs

WalletLite contains no analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs. Your financial data stays on your device.

4. Data Deletion

Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data. You can also clear all data from within the app via Settings → Clear All Data.
