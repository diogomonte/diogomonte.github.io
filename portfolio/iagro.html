IAgro

Farm finance manager for Android — offline, simple, and built for the field.

Android Kotlin Compose

What is IAgro?

IAgro is a lightweight farm finance manager built for Android. It lets farmers and rural producers track their financial activity — from animal pricing and livestock-related costs to general farm expenses — all from a single local app.

The app was designed to be as simple and practical as possible. No internet connection. No account creation. No complexity. Just open the app, enter your data, and track what matters.

Features

  • Track income and expenses related to farm activities
  • Manage pricing for animals and livestock
  • Record general agricultural costs and revenues
  • View a clear summary of your farm finances
  • Fully offline — works anywhere, even without signal

Privacy

IAgro stores all data locally on your device. There is no data collection, no analytics, and no internet access. Your financial information never leaves your phone.

Built With

  • Kotlin — primary language
  • Jetpack Compose — modern declarative UI
  • Room — local database for on-device storage
